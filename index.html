What is Uproot

A platform where communities take root.

Uproot is a modern, community-driven platform designed to help neighbors organize, connect, and take meaningful action on climate change. Whether you're starting a local garden, planning a cleanup, or launching a service project — Uproot gives you the tools to make it happen.

We believe that the most powerful climate solutions start at the grassroots level. When communities come together, small actions multiply into lasting change.

  • Form public or private community groups
  • Plan and manage local events with RSVP tracking
  • Organize volunteer service projects
  • Connect with like-minded neighbors
Why Uproot

Because the planet can't wait.

The climate crisis is the defining challenge of our time. But individual action can feel small. Uproot changes that by turning individual efforts into collective impact.

We built Uproot because we believe that real, lasting change happens when communities are empowered to act together — with the right tools, the right people, and the right purpose.
